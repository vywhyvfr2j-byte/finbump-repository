# FinBump — Full Feature Video Recording Guide

Step-by-step recording scripts for **every feature** in the app, grouped the same way the bottom-nav "More" sheet groups them. Follow each script on a phone (or simulator) to capture a clean screen recording.

## General recording tips (apply to every video)

- Record in **portrait (9:16)** at 1080p.
- Keep each clip **15–30 seconds**; shorter is better.
- Turn on **Do Not Disturb** so notifications don't interrupt.
- Use a demo account that already has **sample people, pets, gifts, notes, and a few completed acts** so screens look lived-in.
- Start each clip already on the feature's main screen — trim login and loading spinners out.
- Smooth, steady taps; avoid long hesitations.
- Keep the bottom nav visible so each clip reads as "in-app."

# Primary tabs

## Home  (`/`)
**Goal:** Show the daily landing screen pulling everything together.
1. Land on Home with the greeting, today's acts, and care reminders loaded.
2. Scroll slowly so the "Today" list, quick-capture box, and date reminders are visible.
3. Tap one act's check-mark to complete it (shows the satisfying check-off).
4. Tap the notification bell so the in-app notification feed opens.
5. Stop recording.

## Acts  (`/acts`)
**Goal:** Show logging and completing a thoughtful act.
1. Open `/acts`.
2. Tap "+ Add act" (`/add-act`), fill in a title, cadence, and a person, then save.
3. Back on Acts, tap the new act's checkbox to complete it.
4. Tap the filter chips (e.g. "Due soon") to show the list re-filtering.
5. Stop recording.

## Partner  (`/profile`)
**Goal:** Show the partner/profile page and editing a preference.
1. Open `/profile`.
2. Scroll so the birthday, anniversary, likes, and love-language fields are visible.
3. Tap **Edit**, change one field (e.g. add a like), and **Save**.
4. Stop recording.

## Calendar / Month Ahead  (`/calendar`)
**Goal:** Show upcoming occasions and reminders in one view.
1. Open `/calendar`.
2. Scroll through the month so occasion dots and reminder chips are visible.
3. Tap one occasion chip to open its detail/occasion row.
4. Stop recording.

## Explore  (`/find-it`)
**Goal:** Show the Explore hub as the gateway to every feature.
1. Open `/find-it`.
2. Scroll slowly through the feature tiles so each group is visible.
3. Tap one tile (e.g. "Who You Love") to show it navigating to that feature.
4. Stop recording.

5. Stop recording.

## Month Ahead  (`/occasions`)
1. Open `/occasions`.
2. Scroll through the month sections so each occasion row is visible.
3. Tap an occasion to open its gift/occasion sheet.
4. Stop recording.

## Groups  (`/groups`)
1. Open `/groups`.
2. Tap a group to open its detail page (`/groups/:id`).
3. Show the member list and any bulk-message action.
4. Stop recording.

## Pets  (`/pets`)
1. Open `/pets`.
2. Tap a pet to open its profile panel.
3. Show the birthday/gotcha day and care reminders.
4. Stop recording.

# Connect & Express

## Cards  (`/cards`)
1. Open `/cards`.
2. Tap "New card" (`/card-builder`).
3. Pick an occasion and a recipient, choose a message, and preview.
4. Tap **Send** to deliver in-app.
5. Stop recording.

## Grab a Meal  (`/meals`)
1. Open `/meals` and tap "New gathering".
2. Fill in title, area, cuisine, budget, and add 2–3 participants.
3. Tap **Send invites** / **Create**.
4. Open the meal detail (`/meals/:id`), then tap **"Suggest a plan"** to show the AI picking a restaurant.
5. Let the AI plan card (venue, rationale, fit score) render.
6. Stop recording.

## Message Writer  (`/message-writer`)
1. Open `/message-writer`.
2. Select an occasion and recipient.
3. Enter a tone hint / keywords (e.g. "funny, warm").
4. Tap **Write message** and let the generated text appear.
5. Tap **Use this** / copy into the card preview; optionally **Regenerate** once.
6. Stop recording.

## Relationship Notes  (`/relationship-notes`)
1. Open `/relationship-notes`.
2. Scroll through shared goals and private notes.
3. Tap a goal to toggle it complete, and add a new note.
4. Stop recording.

## Memory Moments  (`/memory`)
1. Open `/memory`.
2. Tap the evening check-in prompt and type a one-line moment.
3. Optionally attach a photo.
4. Save so it lands in the memory timeline.
5. Scroll the timeline so prior moments are visible.
6. Stop recording.
